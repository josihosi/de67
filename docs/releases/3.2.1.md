# de67 3.2.1 — GPT-6 update

You bring the idea. A team of Codex agents plans, builds, tests, and keeps going—now with GPT-6 Luna, Sol, and Astra.

- Refreshing a partial worker roster could drop previously probed Sol and Astra choices. The coordinator can again choose Luna, Sol, or Astra workers from the available roster.

- Stronger supervisor and worker handoffs
