# de67 3.3.0 — Keep the work moving

You bring the idea. A team of Codex agents plans, builds, tests, and keeps going—now with GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Astra.

## New in 3.3

- GPT-6.1 Sol for coordination and Sol workers, with Luna and Astra retained
- Unfinished work, accepted evidence, and original task clocks survive administrative restarts
- Compact exact-task context, with full history and proof retrieval available
- Missing historical delivery projections recover without crashing the supervisor
- Distinct Discord follow-up replies arrive in order
- SQLite handles close reliably and Windows uses the installed ripgrep target
