# WEC

*User intent and language brief*

## User outcome

What the user wants to be able to do or experience.

## Intended experience

The important user-visible flow, feel, and success conditions.

## Project language and terminology

Preferred names, vocabulary, tone, and any owner-chosen programming or interface language.

## Boundaries

What belongs in scope, what does not, and what must remain unchanged.

## Decisions

Settled user choices and the reason for any non-obvious choice.

## Prototype or reaction questions

Questions that require a concrete example, prototype, or user reaction rather than more discussion.

## Handoff to DE-67-2

Relevant repositories or artifacts and any caution the specification author must preserve.
